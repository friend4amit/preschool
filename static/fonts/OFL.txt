These are the `latin` and `latin-ext` subsets of the variable fonts, taken from
Google Fonts and self-hosted deliberately: loading them from fonts.gstatic.com is a
third-party request that logs the IP of every parent who opens the site, which is
what docs/plan.md rules out under the DPDP Act. Self-hosting also removes a
render-blocking cross-origin connection on a slow mobile connection.

To refresh, request the variable ranges (not fixed weights) so one file serves every
weight:

  https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Fraunces:opsz,wght@9..144,100..900&family=Karla:wght@200..800&display=swap

fetched with a modern browser User-Agent, then download the latin and latin-ext
woff2 files it names.
