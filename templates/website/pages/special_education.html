Thoughtful education

Every child learns differently

An inclusive preschool is not one that treats every child the same. It is one that notices what each child needs, and adjusts.

Noticing early, gently

The early years are when differences in speech, movement, attention or social comfort first become visible. Noticing is not diagnosing — it is paying attention, and telling you what we see.

Working with families, not around them

You know your child better than we do. Anything we notice, we discuss with you first and privately — and we will never label a child.

Adjusting the room, not the child

A quieter corner, a longer transition, a different way to join in. Most of what an inclusive classroom needs is small, practical, and costs nothing.
